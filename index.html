How it works

Three steps. No US bank visit, no remittance-center lines, no "processing fee" surprises.

1

Send from the US

Send USD from your bank account or debit card in the TulayPay app — built for Filipino workers in the US.

2

We convert at wholesale

Your dollars will convert at near-mid-market rates through licensed FX partners — the rate shown up front before you confirm.

3

Family receives pesos

Straight into their e-wallet or bank account, or cash pickup — TulayPay will charge no receive fee on their side. Cash-out or withdrawal fees may apply depending on the wallet operator's policy.

Planned coverage across the Philippines

Not GCash-only. TulayPay is being built to deliver into every major Philippine e-wallet, plus bank accounts and cash pickup nationwide.

GCash Maya ShopeePay GrabPay Coins.ph (availability at launch) Bank accounts via InstaPay / PESONet Cash pickup
Honest note: TulayPay is in development. Delivery will run on licensed partner rails in the US and the Philippines; partner discussions are underway and coverage confirms at launch. TulayPay will not hold or transmit customer funds; delivery is performed by licensed money transmitters and banks in the US and the Philippines.

More than remittance: our own wallet

TulayPay is also building a GCash-style stored-value wallet — hold USD in the US, pesos in the Philippines, send P2P, buy load, and pay bills — riding licensed banking and e-money partners, upgradable to our own licenses as volume grows.
